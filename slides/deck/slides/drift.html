This is simulated, not field data, and it is one kind of damage. Say so. We found both problems while building this. The first version used PSI over 24 hours. Healthy traffic diluted the drifted photos, so no alert fired. Then we measured the conventional 0.25 threshold on small windows, and it false-alarmed more than half the time. An alarm that always rings gets ignored. The simple count of rejected or unsure photos worked far better. Lesson: measure your monitoring the same way you measure your model.
